1. Who is responsible

The data controller is the site operator. Contact for any privacy matter (including to reach the data protection officer): the operator's contact.

We do not ask for your name, CPF (Brazilian taxpayer ID), phone number, address or card or bank data. We do not use the data for advertising and we do not sell data.

3. Cookies and third-party sites

  • We use one essential cookie (lh_session) to keep you signed in. It does not track you and is not used for advertising. Without it you cannot sign in.
  • We do not use analytics or advertising cookies.
  • The pages load fonts and styles from Google Fonts and Tailwind CSS (CDN). When you open the site, your browser makes requests to these services, which receive your IP address under their own policies.

4. Who we share data with

  • Pix payment provider (for example, Mercado Pago, when enabled): receives the amount and the order code to generate and confirm the Pix. We do not send your real email: we use a technical address instead. The provider processes your payment data under its own policy.
  • Email provider: sends the confirmation and password recovery codes to your email.
  • Hosting and infrastructure of the site.
  • Authorities, when the law requires.
  • Other visitors see only your nickname, the amounts and minutes of the bids and the live image of the game. We never show your email.

5. Video and controls

The image and sound of the game are streamed live to the browser. We do not record the stream or what you press. The buttons of the current player pass through the server only to reach the console.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account: until you delete it (Account screen, with your password, and with no tickets in the queue). Accounts that never confirmed the email are deleted within 2 hours.
  • Confirmation and password codes: valid for 15 minutes and deleted once used.
  • Session: up to 30 days, or until you sign out.
  • Order records (amount, date, status): kept for the period the law requires for financial records, even after the account is deleted, without your nickname or email.
  • Password attempt counters: deleted automatically after 24 hours without use.

7. Your rights

You can ask, at any time, for: confirmation that we process your data, access to it, correction, anonymization or deletion, portability, information about who we share it with, and withdrawal of consent. To delete everything, use Account → Delete account; for other requests, write to the operator's contact. We reply within 15 days. You can also complain to the ANPD (Autoridade Nacional de Proteção de Dados, Brazil's National Data Protection Authority).

8. Security

Passwords are stored as a hash; login uses an HttpOnly cookie; there is a lockout after 5 wrong passwords and limits against abuse; email codes are stored only as a hash. No system is 100% secure: if there is an incident that puts you at risk, we will notify you and the ANPD as the law requires.

9. Minors and changes

The service is only for people over 18; if we learn of an account belonging to a minor, we will delete it. If this policy changes, the date at the top changes and, for important changes, we announce it on the site.
