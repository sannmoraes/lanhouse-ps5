3. How the queue (auction) works

  • You place a bid for 5 to 60 minutes (in steps of 5) with an amount in whole reais. Whoever offers more per minute plays first; in a tie, whoever placed the bid first goes first.
  • A new bid never interrupts whoever is already playing: it only changes the order of those who are waiting.
  • The position shown is a forecast: another bid can change it at any time.
  • You can raise a bid that is still waiting and you pay only the difference. The raise only counts after the payment is confirmed; a pending Pix changes neither your position nor your bid. If your turn has already started when the Pix arrives, the extra minutes become a new bid; if the raise was only in amount, it buys nothing and the amount is refunded (see item 5).
  • The free queue gives short turns (currently 10 minutes), one spot per account, with a wait between one turn and the next, and it joins the rotation with the paid queue. The limits may change; the current value appears on the screen.
  • If more than one person is in the queue, the turn ends when the time ends, even in the middle of a match.

4. Payment

Payment is by Pix, in advance, for the full amount of the bid (or of the difference, for a raise). The bid only joins the queue after the payment provider confirms the Pix. A Pix paid after the QR code deadline is still honored. We do not store your bank details: the Pix is processed by the payment provider.

5. Time, failures and refunds

  • Once your turn has started, time already used is not refunded, including for lag, a lost match or a change of mind, unless the law says otherwise. These terms do not limit the rights that consumer protection law gives you.

  • If you are removed from the queue for breaking these rules, there is no automatic refund; we review each case individually.
